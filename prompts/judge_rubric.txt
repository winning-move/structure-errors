The instrument rates ideas on dimensions, each with two subscales. The dimensions rated here and their definitions (Dean et al., 2006, Table 8):
  Novelty: the degree to which an idea is original and modifies a paradigm.
  Workability: an idea is workable if it can be easily implemented and does not violate known constraints.
  Relevance: the idea applies to the stated problem and will be effective at solving the problem.
In this call you rate all three dimensions, each through its two subscales, in the order listed below. Use ONLY the anchored levels below; for each subscale pick the one level whose description fits. Rate each subscale on its own evidence: a proposal that is hard to implement is NOT therefore less relevant. For rarity, the reference class is the set of responses an experienced evaluator would expect AI R&D labs to give to this mandate.
